## 2. Rules, topic by topic

### Platform
- **WInS (Wharton Investment Simulator)**, run by StockTrak (vendor "Stock-Trak"). Login: https://edu.stocktrak.com/wharton/ . [OFFICIAL 26-27, sources 3, 9]
- **One shared account per team**, one username/password for all members. [OFFICIAL 26-27, sources 1, 6]
- SurveyMonkey Apply is the hub for case, deliverables and updates. [OFFICIAL 26-27]
- Practice portfolios were wiped before official trading began Sep 28. [OFFICIAL 26-27]

### Starting cash
- **$300,000** virtual cash for the official trading period. [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 1]
- Source 1 also states: "The additional $150,000 contribution described in the Client Case Study will not be added to WInS", and that WInS gains/losses must NOT be added to or subtracted from the client's long-term projections; projections must use the case-study cash flows and "reasonable return assumptions consistent with your strategy". [OFFICIAL 26-27, sources 1, 3]
  - This matches the repo: `CONTRIBUTIONS = {2027: 300000, 2028: 150000}`. The WInS $300k = the first contribution (2027). The $150k (2028) exists only in the projection model.
- Not $500k (that was 2025-26) and not $100k (pre-2025-26). Third-party pages claiming $500k for 2026-27 are wrong per source 1.

### Eligible securities
- Official 2026-27 list: **any stock available on WInS; any ETF available on WInS; Treasury bonds available on WInS (US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands).** The page says "for BOTH contributions" (ambiguous wording; the case seems to have two contributions, only the first is in WInS). [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 1]
- **No approved stock list and no approved ETF list are mentioned for 2026-27** ("any ETF available on WInS"). Last season there was an Approved ETF List and an Approved Treasury List. [OFFICIAL 25-26, source 8]
- **Mutual funds are not on the 2026-27 list** (so the repo's mutual-fund proxies VFINX, VEIPX, VBMFX, VFITX, VFICX cannot be held; implement the Funds and Bonds buckets with ETFs and/or Treasuries). [inference from the list above]
- **Minimum stock price:** 2026-27 page does not mention one. 2025-26 had "$5 or higher (or equivalent)". [OFFICIAL 25-26] -> **Comply with >= $5 anyway.**
- **ETF requirement:** 2026-27 page does not mention one. 2025-26 required at least one ETF from the Approved List. [OFFICIAL 25-26] -> **Hold at least one mainstream ETF anyway** (the Funds/Bonds buckets need ETFs in WInS regardless).
- **ADRs / OTC / foreign listings:** [UNCONFIRMED]. Source 1 says international equity orders "may experience a longer delay" and clear at the end of the next trading day, and WInS converts currency automatically. Safest: US exchange-listed (NYSE/Nasdaq/NYSE American) common stock only, ticker must be searchable in WInS.
- **Not permitted last season (official 2025-26):** ETFs outside the approved list, margin, short selling, stock-secured debt, crypto, derivatives (options, futures, swaps), anything not on the approved list. [OFFICIAL 25-26, source 8]. The 2026-27 page is silent but lists only stocks/ETFs/Treasuries, so options/crypto/shorting are effectively out. The 2020 WInS guide also shows margin, short selling and day trading are "not permitted" class settings. [source 9] -> **Treat all of these as banned.**

### Position limits / diversification / number of holdings
- **No required sector allocation or minimum number of sectors.** Diversification is "an important investment principle"; consider asset classes, sectors, industries, market caps, regions, risk characteristics, funding purposes. "The goal is to build an intentional mix of investments appropriate for your strategy, not simply to own a large number of securities." [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 1]
- **No max-% per stock and no min number of holdings are published.** [UNCONFIRMED]. But WInS has a configurable per-stock "Position Limit" (example in the 2020 guide: 25% of portfolio) that Wharton sets per season. [source 9] -> **Open WInS > My Portfolio > Portfolio Summary and read the "trading rules" panel at the bottom** (position limit, day trading, short/margin, commission). That panel is the ground truth.
- Judges do score "appropriate diversification" (section 4).

### Trading limits and costs
- **Up to 200 trades** over the competition. [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 1] (A third party says each buy or sell counts as one trade. [3RD PARTY] Assume that.)
- **Each trade may not exceed twice the security's current daily trading volume.** [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 1]
- **Commissions:** not mentioned on any 2026-27 official page. Wharton sets "commission charges" as a WInS parameter [source 9]. Third parties report $25 per stock trade and $10 per Treasury bond, charged only when a trade clears. [3RD PARTY, and a school write-up] [UNCONFIRMED for 2026-27]. WInS shows the commission on the "Preview" screen before you click "Confirm". [source 9] -> Budget $25/trade as the worst case: 40 trades = $1,000 (0.33% of $300k).
- Interest on cash: [UNCONFIRMED].
- Fractional shares: [UNCONFIRMED]; StockTrak order tickets are in whole shares in the guide. Plan whole shares.
- Order types in WInS: Market, Limit, Stop, Trailing Stop. [source 9, 2020]

### Short selling / margin / options
- Banned in 2025-26 [OFFICIAL 25-26]; 2026-27 page silent; WInS class settings historically disallow. -> **Not allowed; do not use.**

### Execution timing
- U.S. market 9:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. ET, weekdays. **Orders placed while open execute at real-time prices. Orders placed while closed fill at the opening price when the market reopens.** Bid/ask shown in real time; **open-position prices on screen may be 10-15 minutes delayed.** [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 1]
- A buy executes only if fewer shares than actually traded that day are being bought (the volume cap). Limit/stop orders execute only when their conditions are met. [source 9]
- International equity and bond orders are processed at the end of the applicable trading day (an order at 4:00 p.m. Monday may clear end of Tuesday). [OFFICIAL 26-27]
- Consequence for Wed Oct 21: an order entered before 9:30 a.m. ET fills at the Oct 21 open price (no price control with a market order); an order entered after the open fills at live prices. Either is fine; just do not expect a particular price.

### Trading window and freeze
- Official trading: **Mon Sep 28, 2026 through Fri Nov 6, 2026.** On Nov 6 (when the IPS is due, 5:00 p.m. ET) **the portfolio is frozen: no further trades or changes.** [OFFICIAL 26-27, sources 1, 2, 3]
- Not Dec 4 / Dec 5. Dec 4 is the Final Report due date; some third-party pages show a Dec 5 trading end, which is wrong per sources 1-3.
- From Wed Oct 21 to Fri Nov 6 inclusive there are **13 US trading days** and no market holidays. After Nov 6 the book is locked, so the Oct 21 trades will be most of what the judges see of implementation.
- An order placed after the Nov 6 close would fill at the next open (Mon Nov 9), after the freeze, so **treat the last safe order time as market hours on Thu Nov 5.** [inference]

### "First trade" eligibility
- 2025-26 required at least one executed trade by Fri Oct 10, 2025 4:00 p.m. ET to stay eligible. [OFFICIAL 25-26, source 8]
- **No 2026-27 first-trade deadline appears on any official 2026-27 page I read.** One third-party page claims "Oct 10, 2026, 4 p.m. ET"; Oct 10, 2026 is a **Saturday**, so that is almost certainly a carry-over error. [3RD PARTY, UNCONFIRMED]
- The rules page does say teams "must meet the required trading activity and portfolio management guidelines throughout the competition" and that detailed trading requirements were sent only to registered teams via SurveyMonkey Apply on Sep 15. [OFFICIAL 26-27, source 6] -> **ACTION: check the Sep 15 announcement/inbox on SurveyMonkey Apply for a minimum-activity or first-trade requirement. If the team has not made any trade yet, make sure that is allowed. If unsure, ask the program through the portal's "Contact" link before Wed.**

## 4. How judging works

Official statements:
- "Success is not determined by portfolio performance." [source 5]
- WInS standings "have little to do with the final outcome"; "Winners are selected on the strength and articulation of their overall investment strategies." [FAQ, source 7]
- **You are NOT evaluated on:** portfolio ranking, number of trades, outperforming other teams, or whether the portfolio makes money. WInS "provides evidence of the decisions your team made and how you put your strategy into practice." [source 3]
- **Evaluated deliverables:** Trading Notes Analysis, Investment Policy Statement, Final Report (the Roster and School Documentation are compliance items). Top 50 semifinalists are chosen from the **IPS and the Final Report**. [sources 2, 6]
- Semifinal/final panels (last season): Wharton student evaluators + asset managers (Aberdeen Investments) for the Top 50; a 5-person industry panel for the finale, citing "analytical thinking, creativity, and a deep understanding of the client's goals". [sources 10, 11, 2025-26]

**Official 2026-27 evaluation criteria (verbatim themes from source 2):**
1. **Investment Strategy:** clear and creative investment thesis; disciplined planning across Laura's **changing time horizons and cash-flow needs**; **appropriate diversification**; **consistency with the team's IPS**.
2. **Client Knowledge and Objectives:** tailors the strategy to Laura's circumstances, priorities, **risk considerations**, and **residency goals**; thoughtful understanding of the client; recommendations that can earn her confidence.
3. **Portfolio Analysis:** effective use of investment concepts and tools; integrates quantitative and qualitative analysis; **reasonable assumptions and projections to evaluate funding reliability, the facility contribution, and financial flexibility under varying market outcomes**.
4. **Articulation of Competition Experience:** teamwork, communication, learning; explains research and decision process; reflects on growth and response to challenges.
5. **Creativity and Presentation:** compelling narrative in an authentic team voice; data used effectively; original thinking; communicates **Laura's potential facility contribution and investment uncertainty** clearly and credibly to **prospective co-sponsors**.

Answers to the specific questions:
- Is judging based on rationale and process rather than raw returns? **Yes**, explicitly.
- Does it weigh the client case and risk management? **Yes**: criteria 1-3 and 5 are client-, risk-, funding-reliability- and uncertainty-focused. Exact weightings are not published. [UNCONFIRMED: numeric weights]
- Note the pairing: the IPS (due Nov 6) is the "cornerstone"; the portfolio must be **consistent with the IPS**, and each trade should have "a clear purpose within that strategy."

## 7. Checklist for any final trade list (tick every box before placing orders)

**A. Hard constraints (official 2026-27)**
- [ ] Every security is a US-listed stock, an ETF, or a Treasury bond that **appears in the WInS search** (no mutual funds, no crypto, no options/futures, no other).
- [ ] Total buys + commissions <= **$300,000** WInS cash (keep a small buffer, e.g. 0.5% = $1,500, for commissions and open-price drift).
- [ ] Planned trade count well under **200** (a 40-60 trade plan leaves room; every buy and sell counts).
- [ ] Each order <= **2x the stock's current daily volume** (shares). Practically: skip names whose average daily dollar volume is not many multiples of the order size (suggest ADV >= $5M for a ~$4-8k order).
- [ ] Orders will be entered before **Fri Nov 6** (last safe order time: market hours Thu Nov 5). No order should rely on after-close entry.
- [ ] Orders entered pre-open (before 9:30 a.m. ET Wed) will fill at the **open price**; orders during the day fill at live prices. Expect no fill price guarantee with market orders; use limit orders only if the team wants price control (and accept that unfilled limits do not count as executed trades).
- [ ] A **Trading Note** (reasoning, research, role in strategy, key risks) exists for every decision, written at the time of the decision. Three will be picked for the **Oct 23** Trading Notes Analysis.
- [ ] Trades are placed by students through the one shared account; no advisor decision-making.

**B. Carry-over rules (not restated for 2026-27, cost nothing to follow)**
- [ ] Share price >= **$5** at order time (prefer > $10 so a dip cannot cross below $5).
- [ ] At least **one ETF** in the portfolio.
- [ ] No short sales, no margin, no derivatives, no crypto, no stock-secured debt, no same-day round trips (day trading is a WInS class setting that has historically been off).
- [ ] No OTC/pink-sheet names; prefer plain US common stock listed on NYSE/Nasdaq/NYSE American; avoid foreign-listed shares (end-of-next-day processing, FX effects).

**C. WInS-specific checks to do on screen (these supersede anything above)**
- [ ] Open My Portfolio > Portfolio Summary > rules panel: read **position limit %, day trading, short/margin flags, commission**. If a position limit exists (e.g. 25%), no ticker may exceed it.
- [ ] Confirm the **commission** on the "Preview" screen of the first order.
- [ ] Confirm the **cash balance is $300,000** before trading.

**D. Judging-driven design rules (soft but scored)**
- [ ] Every holding has a one-line **role** in the IPS (growth, liquidity, funding reliability, flexibility, risk control).
- [ ] The portfolio is **consistent with the IPS** and with the case cash flows (this is an explicit criterion).
- [ ] Diversification is **intentional, not maximal**: do not buy 60 tiny positions just to look diversified. Aim for roughly 12-25 stocks in the stock sleeve plus a few ETFs/Treasuries; spread across GICS sectors and not all one factor.
- [ ] Do not describe WInS P&L as evidence for the client's projections.
- [ ] Holding window after Oct 21 is only 13 trading days, so do not judge or justify picks on short-run returns.

## 8. How the repo's case context should shape the stock picks

Case facts as modeled in the repo (consistent with the official pages: $300k WInS balance, $150k not in WInS, "Laura", "facility contribution", "co-sponsors", "reserve"):
- Contributions: **$300,000 start of 2027, $150,000 start of 2028** (`CONTRIBUTIONS`).
- **Operating reserve:** 10 payments of $50,000 from the start of 2033 (2033-2042). Treasury ladder at the 3.5% planning yield costs about **$430,384** in 2033 (my calculation from `PLANNING_YIELD`), and `RESERVE_MIX` has **0% stocks** with `RESERVE_RISKY_START = 0` (pure Treasury ladder). So **stocks are not funding the reserve**; they only add upside toward the facility contribution.
- **Facility target** $100,000 with a 20% flexibility haircut means about **$555,384** of portfolio value needed in 2033 (reserve + $100k/0.8). `SAFETY_PROB = 0.99` on covering the reserve, `GOAL_PROB = 0.65` on the facility target.
- **Baseline split** 37.5% Bonds / 37.5% Funds / **25% Stocks**; stock bound 10%-40%; **`MAX_VOL = 0.10`** (portfolio annual volatility cap even when chasing the target).
- Time horizon to the reserve: about 6 years (2033).

What follows for the stock sleeve (my reasoning; verify with the repo tools):

1. **Dollar size.** If WInS mirrors the policy split, the stock sleeve is **25% x $300,000 = $75,000** (Funds 37.5% = $112,500 and Bonds 37.5% = $112,500 implemented via ETFs/Treasuries because mutual funds are not tradable in WInS). For 12-25 names that is about $3,000-$6,000 per stock (1.0%-2.0% of the WInS balance). This keeps any single stock far below any plausible position limit and below ~2% of the case portfolio.
2. **Volatility budget is tight.** Using the repo's own proxy data (`data/*.csv`, daily returns through 2026-10-13, my read-only calculation): bucket volatilities are Bonds about 4.7%, Funds about 17.4%, placeholder Stocks (JNJ/PG/KO/PEP) about 16.6%; Funds-vs-Stocks correlation 0.65. At the 37.5/37.5/25 baseline, portfolio volatility is already about **9.7%**, against `MAX_VOL` of 10%. Sensitivity (same weights, sleeve-vs-Funds correlation 0.65 to 0.80): a stock sleeve at 20% vol gives about 10.5-10.9% total, at 25% vol about 11.6-12.1%, at 30% vol about 12.7-13.3% (at correlation 0.90 add roughly another 0.3 points). The break-even sleeve volatility is roughly **15-18%** depending on correlation with the Funds bucket (0.65 -> 17.8%, 0.80 -> 16.0%, 0.90 -> 14.9%).
   - A momentum or deep-value sleeve typically runs hotter than 16-18%. So either (a) **tilt the sleeve to lower-volatility, high-quality, diversified names**, (b) **cut the stock weight toward the 10-20% range** allowed by `BOUNDS` and make up the difference with Funds/Bonds, or (c) accept and openly disclose that the sleeve breaches `MAX_VOL`. Judges reward "risk considerations", so choose (a) or (b) and show the arithmetic.
   - To get the official number: put the real tickers in `config.py` `BUCKETS["Stocks"]` and run `python3 allocate.py 2027 300000` (the repo uses its own monthly-block volatility, so figures will differ slightly from mine).
3. **Sleeve is a 6-year compounding allocation, not a 3-week trade.** Prefer liquid US large/mid caps with durable quality; avoid micro-caps and anything near $5 (Magic Formula screens often surface small, volatile, thin names; apply price >= $5 or better >= $10, market cap and ADV filters first).
4. **Blend the three strategies for diversification, then check overlap.** Quality-filtered momentum, Magic Formula and GICS-comps valuation can all load on the same sector or factor at once; cap any GICS sector at a stated limit (for example no sector above ~25-30% of the sleeve) and cap any single stock (for example <= 5% of the sleeve, about $3,750). The IPS should state these limits so the portfolio demonstrably follows it.
5. **Reserve safety first.** Anything that can threaten the Treasury-ladder reserve (stocks 0% of reserve) is out of scope for the stock sleeve; explain in the IPS that equity risk is confined to the growth/facility portion, consistent with `SAFETY_PROB = 0.99`.
6. **Funding reliability and flexibility.** Criterion 3 asks about "funding reliability, the facility contribution, and financial flexibility under varying market outcomes." Keep ample liquidity (high-ADV names, plus ETFs and Treasuries) so the portfolio can be rebalanced or the contribution adjusted without market impact; mention the 20% flexibility reserve (`FLEX_PCT`).
7. **Non-financial client preferences.** The criteria mention Laura's "priorities", "risk considerations" and "residency goals". I could not read the case PDF, so any exclusions or preferences in it (sectors to avoid, mission alignment, concentration limits, liquidity needs, ESG) are [UNCONFIRMED]. Apply them as hard screens before the strategy screens run.
8. **Do not extrapolate WInS results.** The official rules say WInS gains and losses must not feed the long-term projections; keep using the model's data-driven return assumptions.
