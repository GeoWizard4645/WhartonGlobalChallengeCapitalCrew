# Spec-compliance audit: three strategies vs the team doc and the research specs

Auditor: independent read of the code plus numerical checks on synthetic and real data (scripts in the session scratch dir `audit_spec/`, no repo code changed, no git). Data as of the cached panel ending 2026-10-16. Existing unit tests for technical, quality, comps, key metrics and the engine were run: 196 passed.

No finding is HIGH: nothing found changes the picks or returns materially in the way a spec violation would. The three medium items are a data-layer share-count bug (names silently dropped), a stale QM pre-registration, and a labelling issue (MF 'N' versus names actually held). Details in section 2.

## 4. Numerical edge-case checks (all passed unless a finding says otherwise)

- Division by ~0: every ratio goes through a positive-denominator guard (`_ratio`, `where(den > 0)`, `_safe_div`); ROE with tiny positive equity is huge but then fails D/E. MF ROC uses a 1%-of-assets capital floor (LGND ROC 15.4 is a floored case, flagged); EY with EV just above 0 is the one unguarded extreme (picks with EY > 40% in 40 of 181 months, at most 2 per month; the live table flags them).
- NaN propagation: no gate passes on NaN. QM `ciks.map(s).fillna(False)`, `NaN > x` is False; MF `np.isfinite(roc) & np.isfinite(ey)` guard; TC gates `fillna(False)`. Checked for leverage, FCF, margin and ROIC: 0 NaN passes. Exclusion flags (`recent_ipo`, `recent_merger`) default to False when their inputs are NaN, i.e. a name with missing acquisition/share data is not excluded (rare; recent IPOs are also caught by the 253-day price-history rule in QM).
- Ties: QM momentum ties by ticker; MF rank ties averaged, final tie-break EY then market cap (mergesort); TC score rounded to 1e-10, then lower ND/EBITDA, then CIK. All deterministic.
- Sign conventions: snapshot capex is a positive outflow (median > 0), so CFO - capex is right; ACC = (CFO - NI)/A (higher = better), LEV = -debt/A, BAB = -beta, discount = 1 - x/benchmark (positive = cheap). Correlations of QMJ-lite composites with their parts have the expected signs.
- Units: percentages are decimals everywhere (stop 0.15, vol 0.20, theta 0.15, ERP 0.05, DGS10 0.0434); RSI is 0-100 with band (50, 70); fundamentals are in USD (AAPL net income 100.4B); `slope_pp` is x100 against a -0.5 tolerance; quality percentiles 0-100 with `Q1-50` => 50.
- ND/EBITDA when EBITDA <= 0: undefined and fails. P/E when NI <= 0: invalid, never zero-filled. Beta: quality.py daily 252-day and key_metrics.py weekly 104-week betas match a manual OLS to 4 decimals.
- Look-ahead: fundamentals use `filed < t`, universe and GICS are as of the snapshot, signals at the close of t, trades at t+1 close; vol scalar and stop use only past data. The `split_factor_after` use to undo later splits restores the as-traded scale and does not leak information.

## 5. Things the team should say in the write-up

1. **Survivorship is the largest threat to every number.** Only 64% of the point-in-time S&P 900 has a Yahoo price series in 2011 (572 of 896), 74% in 2016, 86% in 2019, 99.9% in 2026. The missing names are the delisted and acquired ones, so value strategies (MF, TC) are probably flattered most, and early-year results are the least reliable. Use the same-universe equal-weight benchmark, as the repo does.
2. **MF micro-cap rule.** The doc's '<50 million' is a typo for 'exclude below $50M'; the code does that, but inside the S&P 900 the rule never bites (2-4 names at the dates checked, all data errors). The binding floors are $1B (primary) and $5B.
3. **MF 'hold 20-30'** is names per cohort; with 12 overlapping cohorts the staggered book holds about 57 names (F3).
4. **QM 'quality'** is Q3a: TTM profit > 0, debt/equity <= 1, and gross margin OR ROE at or above the industry median; ~25% of names have no gross margin and pass only via ROE; 5-7% have no mapped debt and fail. The default cell holds 9.7 names on average and is in cash for part of 6% of months (29% at N=20).
5. **QM trend-break exit** = month-end re-screen; vol-scaling = back-cast own-book 126-day vol, 20% target, capped at 100% (no leverage), recomputed monthly; the 'scale up when volatility is low' part of the doc is limited to returning to full exposure. Default cell one-way turnover is ~7.8x per year (1.5% per year at 10 bp).
6. **TC** peers are thin in the S&P 900: ~58% of targets need the industry fallback, ~60% of names valid on both multiples get no benchmark at all, and the pipeline holds 16.7 names on average (6 to 20). 'Both multiples' = AND. 'Without size filter' also removes the 4-5 peer limit. Forward multiples are a live cross-check only.
7. **Key metrics** are target-only gates. Margin stability (slope >= -0.5 pp/yr over 4 years, TTM within 1 pp) is the binding one (about 55% pass); 14 of 648 names with negative invested capital pass the ROIC test automatically.
8. Honest search count: QM 216 cells, MF 18 + 9 ablations, TC 54 + follow-ups; grid.run_grid logs every evaluated cell in the ledger (the DSR trial count); log the ablations and TC follow-ups there too.
